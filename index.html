About Linda

Add photo

Linda Debra was a remarkable woman whose warmth, grace, and unwavering love touched the lives of everyone fortunate enough to know her.

Born on January 1, 1960, Linda grew up with a heart full of compassion and a spirit that could light up any room. She was a devoted mother, a loving wife, a loyal friend, and a pillar of strength for her family and community.

Her infectious laughter, gentle wisdom, and selfless nature left an indelible mark on all who crossed her path. Linda believed in the power of kindness and lived her life as a testament to that belief every single day.

Though she is no longer with us in body, her spirit lives on in the countless lives she touched and the memories she created. She will be forever cherished, deeply missed, and lovingly remembered.
